## Describe the game they thought they were building

Someone opens the app because they want a **shot at the pot** — not because they want a skill tree.

They **watch ads** (attention = chips). Each completed ad is a **ticket with a number**. Other people are doing the same. The room **fills**. You can leave; it still fills. Almost there. Heart rate up.

Then the **show**: not a menu transition — a **lottery climax**. Your tickets are marked. The draw hunts. Near-miss on yours. Or it hits and for one second you’re the person who took everyone’s ad money (even if virtual for now).

You think: **one more ad. one more ticket.**

If luck failed, *maybe* your fighter still took the pit, or you got stronger for next time. That’s the aftertaste.  
**The meal is the number.**

Cash later makes it sharper. Guilds later make it social.  
**Without the draw thrill, nothing else matters.**

---

## Boss: force the product to produce this quote

> “I watched the ads, I held #3 and #7, the room filled, the show started, #7 almost landed, then #4 hit — I need one more ticket.”

### Work that serves the quote
- Ticket = meaningful purchase (ad theater, cost, multi-ticket greed)
- Fill bar / almost-full / push as drama
- Draw ceremony as longest loudest personal moment
- Coins as jackpot identity (not dead UI)
- Near-miss design

### Work that betrays the quote (do not prioritize)
- Fight longer than draw
- Upgrade-first onboarding
- Room unlock ladders in the face of new users
- Coins with no sink and no fantasy
- Instant “PLAY” that skips the *feeling* of buying a ticket and waiting for the pit
