# Pot & Arena — Simple Plan

## The game (one breath)

Watch ads (or bet coins/gems) to enter rooms. When the room fills, two things happen:

1. **Luck** — someone wins the **coin pot**
2. **Fight** — fighters clash (stats + a bit of RNG) for **gems**

You can leave the app anytime; progress is saved. Push when it’s almost full / done. Rewatch the draw + fight + logs later.

**Coins** = jackpots / stakes (harder; future real-$ path locked off)  
**Gems** = upgrades / cosmetics / battle progress  
**No city builder.** Badges + looks + fighters instead.

---

## Leaderboards (Wave 1 — from the start)

| Period | Resets |
|--------|--------|
| Daily | Every day |
| Weekly | Every week |
| Monthly | Every month |
| All-time | Never |

Boards: **Pot King** (coins from pots), **Arena** (gems/arena wins), **Fame** (combined).

Rank badges: Top 1 / 10 / 100 per period → frames (no cash until legal).

Score events written on every match resolve.

## Build waves

| Wave | What |
|------|------|
| **1 — Solo** | Rooms, ads→tickets, pot, fight, coins/gems, replay, cancel, cosmetics hooks, leaderboards + badges |
