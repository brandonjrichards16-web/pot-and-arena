# Boss agent diagnosis — Pot & Arena

## Pillars of a good mobile game app

1. First 30s dopamine  
2. Core loop (one verb → payoff → again)  
3. Spectacle (draw + fight)  
4. Power fantasy (you are the star)  
5. Juice (motion, buttons, hits)  
6. **Rewards ceremony** (every PLAY pays on screen)  
7. Progression without day-1 dump  
8. Return hook (light)  
9. Clarity (3 taps to fun)  
10. Polish / non-SaaS look  

## Research (subagents)

| Pillar | Verdict |
|--------|---------|

| Juice | Beveled buttons, pulse, hit stack, haptics |
| Rewards | **Biggest gap** — backend pays, client said “Done!” |
