# Fun UI research (agents + web) → what we shipped

## From top games (Coin Master, AFK Arena, match-3, gacha lobbies)

1. **Center stage** — character is the biggest thing, not a card list  
2. **One fat CTA** — Play/Spin owns the thumb (pulse, shine, 3D candy look)  
3. **Resources top-right** — gold/gem pills  
4. **Toy palette** — warm gold/coral, not gray SaaS  
5. **Living backdrop** — art + particles, not flat hex  
6. **Bottom dock** — quiet secondary icons  

## Combat spectacle (auto-brawler)

- Tiered damage numbers, hit flash, lunge, short fights, hero framing, end card  

## Applied in app

- `FunShell` lobby art + sparkles  
- `JuicyButton` bouncing CTA  
- `ResourcePills`  
- Home = stage + PLAY + dock
