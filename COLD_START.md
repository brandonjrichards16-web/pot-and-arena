# Cold start: House fighters + invites

## House fighters
- Free & Ad rooms may auto-fill with labeled **House** bots after a grace period (~12s) once ≥1 human joins.
- Coin pot on those rooms: **human tickets only**.
- Stake (COIN/GEM) rooms: **no bots**.
- Always disclosed in UI (`GET /meta`, room `disclaimer`).

## Invites
- Code per user; share blurb in app.
- Both get gems after invitee’s first completed match.
